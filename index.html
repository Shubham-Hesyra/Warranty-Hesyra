Legal & Regulatory Information
1. Authenticity & Scope

This digital warranty applies exclusively to original custom prostheses precision-milled and fabricated by Hesyra Labs. Coverage commences on the verified laboratory dispatch date and remains active for the full registered warranty term.

2. Covered Conditions
  • Internal material structural failure or fracture under normal physiological mastication.
  • Restoration delamination or loss of marginal integrity resulting from fabrication defect.
3. Exclusions & Limitations
  • Accidental trauma, physical impact, sports injuries, or usage beyond normal mastication.
  • Severe untreated bruxism or clenching without a certified laboratory night guard.
  • Damage to biological supporting structures (abutment tooth fracture, root canal failure, secondary caries, periodontal disease, or bone loss).
  • Chairside clinical costs: Doctor consultations, radiographs, anesthesia, temporization, model fabrication, modifications, and re-cementation charges are strictly excluded.
  • Alterations, grinding, or adjustments performed outside certified clinical protocols.
4. Claim Protocol & Verification

Warranty service must be initiated strictly through the treating dental clinic where the restoration was fitted. Valid claims require: (a) verified digital warranty ID, (b) return of the original damaged prosthesis to Hesyra Labs, and (c) fresh jaw impressions or digital scans with prescribing doctor certification. The patient is required to undergo periodic clinical checkups every six months or as directed by their dental practitioner.

5. Sole Remedy & Jurisdiction

Remedy is strictly limited to authorized laboratory remake or repair. Cash refunds, clinical reimbursements, or consequential claims of any kind will not be entertained. Replacement prostheses may exhibit minor anatomical or shade variations within clinical manufacturing tolerances. All disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of courts in Nagpur / Mumbai, Maharashtra.

1. Data Controller & Lawful Processing

Hesyra Labs operates as data fiduciary under applicable statutory standards (Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 / GDPR). We process personal identifiers (Name, Mobile Number, Email, Device Network Metadata) strictly to execute warranty registration and maintain prosthesis provenance.

2. Clinical & Operational Coordination

To fulfill warranty claims, coordinate clinical support, and verify authenticity, necessary records are securely shared on a confidential need-to-know basis with your treating dental clinic, certified clinicians, and authorized service delivery partners.

3. Zero Commercial Advertising Distribution

Hesyra Labs strictly maintains an uncompromising zero-sale policy: your personal records and clinical data are never sold, rented, monetized, or provided to third-party advertisers or commercial brokers.

4. Network Security & Audit Trails

Public IP addresses, an approximate city/region derived from them, and timestamps are recorded at registration to create tamper-evident verification logs, prevent counterfeit claims, and protect patient identity against fraudulent registration. The approximate location is resolved through a third-party IP lookup service; your name and phone number are never sent to it.
